JBL Filmmaker · The approach

A film that brings you back to the feeling.

A wedding is more than a sequence of beautiful moments. It is the atmosphere, the people, the words, the gestures and all the little details that make the day yours.

I'm Jean-Baptiste Larruchon, a wedding filmmaker based in the Paris region. Through JBL Filmmaker, I create cinematic wedding films with a discreet presence, considered imagery and carefully crafted sound. My aim is to tell your story with honesty and sensitivity, creating a film that still feels meaningful years from now.

Whether you are planning an intimate celebration or a weekend shared with family and friends, I take time to understand the spirit of your wedding before shaping the film around it.

The experience

Discreet filming. Thoughtful storytelling.

The most meaningful moments are often the ones nobody planned.

I work quietly and attentively, allowing moments to unfold naturally rather than turning your wedding into a film set. From the anticipation before the ceremony to the energy of the evening, I look for genuine connections and the atmosphere that makes your celebration unique.

In the edit, moving images, voices, speeches, ambient sounds and music come together to create a coherent story. The result is designed to feel personal, timeless and true to you.

Frequently asked questions

Planning a wedding film in France.

Do you film weddings outside Paris?

Yes. I am based in the Paris region and available for weddings throughout France, including Provence and the French Riviera. I also consider destination weddings abroad.

Can we work with you if we are planning our wedding from abroad?

Of course. We can discuss your plans remotely and communicate in English before the wedding. Once I know your date, location and schedule, we can talk through the practical arrangements.

What is your approach to filming?

My approach is cinematic, elegant and discreet. I aim to capture genuine moments as they happen, while paying attention to composition, natural sound, speeches and the atmosphere of the day.

Which parts of the wedding can be filmed?

Coverage can be tailored to your plans and may include preparations, the ceremony, couple portraits, speeches, dinner and the evening celebration. We will define the schedule together.

Do you travel for destination weddings?

Yes, I am open to destination weddings in France and elsewhere in Europe. Travel and accommodation arrangements are discussed according to the location and duration of your celebration.

How can we check your availability?

Use the contact form and include your wedding date, venue or location, and a few details about your plans. I will get back to you to discuss availability and the next steps.
